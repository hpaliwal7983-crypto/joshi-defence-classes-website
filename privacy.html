Privacy notice

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This notice explains how the Joshi Defence Classes website and student portal use information when students and academy administrators sign in and access learning content.

Information used by the portals

  • Students enter their mobile number and a one-time password to sign in. Student accounts are created by the academy.
  • The single academy administrator enters an email address and password to sign in.
  • The portals use account details, batch enrollment and academy learning records to show the right classes, PDFs and announcements.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, manage student enrollment, provide access to batch content, and operate the academy's learning services. The student portal only shows content for batches linked to the signed-in student's active account. The service records test attempts, saved answers and scores, notice read state, and lesson playback progress so students can resume learning and view results.

Service providers and learning links

Authentication, database and private file services are provided through Supabase. Live and recorded video may open or play through YouTube. The web video player may save a student's lesson ID and watched position to resume playback. Those services process information under their own privacy terms.

Learning access

The current portals provide learning and administration features. They do not offer student checkout, wallet, subscription or payment processing.

Data access and account questions

Student accounts are managed by the academy. For account access, correction or deletion requests, contact the academy through the contact channel it gave you.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Joshi Defence Classes through your existing academy contact channel.
